# Review Guide

This repository contains a small public theory and a deliberately large
falsification record. Review the claim before reviewing the archive.

Roughly 95% of the words here are archive. The claim itself is the README plus
[theory sections 1–4](docs/theory.md); the [glossary](docs/glossary.md)
translates the vocabulary if the register gets in the way. Nothing else is
required reading to review the claim — the ledger exists so that the claim can
be checked, not so that it must be read first.

## Five-minute review

Read the [README](README.md), then answer two questions:

1. Does `3 + 1` clearly distinguish current execution pressures from cross-run
   learning?
2. Is the Infrastructure/Harness boundary based on the controlled object rather
   than a product or feature name?

If either answer is no, open an issue with the smallest counterexample.

## Thirty-minute review

Read:

1. [Theory sections 1–4](docs/theory.md) for the exact core;
2. [Counterarguments](docs/counterarguments.md) for known failures and rival
   candidates;
3. [Evidence matrix](docs/evidence.md) to distinguish documented facts from
   theoretical inference.

Do not review 4C as a complete requirements checklist. The original exhaustive
claim is already rejected.

## Adversarial review

Start with the [final falsification
audit](docs/validation/v1-final-falsification-audit.md), then inspect the
[validation ledger](docs/validation/README.md) and
[reproductions](experiments/README.md).

The highest-value contribution is a counterexample that hits one of these
boundaries:

- a recurring current-execution policy cause outside scarcity, semantic
  difference and temporal dependence;
- cross-run learning that cannot be separated from observation, evaluation,
  application development or model training;
- a Harness operation that the mediated transition model cannot represent;
- an evidence, acceptance or permission decision that does not reduce to
  Epistemic Access, Validity or Authority without information loss;
- a mechanism whose layer ownership cannot be determined from its controlled
  object;
- a simpler rival model with equal or better engineering predictions.

One strong counterexample outweighs every successful mapping in the repository.
